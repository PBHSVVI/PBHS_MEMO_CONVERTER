# Phase 8.4C final visual tuning

Phase 8.4C keeps the accepted Teacher App structure and behaviour while adding two restrained PBHS identity treatments to the authenticated workspace.

The application header now ends with one full-width heritage stripe: 5 px provisional PBHS red, 2 px white, and 5 px accessible PBHS green. The existing gold header accent remains unchanged. This stripe is used once beneath the authenticated header and is not repeated on cards or controls.

The shared `--pbhs-canvas` token is now `#eef1f0`, a light, desaturated institutional neutral that distinguishes the workspace from white content panels. Primary surfaces remain `#ffffff`. Red and green retain their existing functional roles outside the heritage stripe.

The sign-in composition, department workspace hierarchy, filters, form tabs, paper cards, and upload action are unchanged. These colours remain provisional UI colours derived from the supplied 2026 crest, not an official PBHS colour specification.
